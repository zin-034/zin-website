About

A small studio with a large view of what the web can do

Zen AI Studio is a creative digital technology studio based in Afghanistan, working with clients around the world. We combine design, programming and artificial intelligence in one team, so an idea never gets lost between the designer and the developer.

We design interfaces, build and maintain websites and web applications, and add AI where it saves people real time. Distance is not a constraint for us: our clients brief us online, review work online, and get the same attention as if we shared an office.

Create. Innovate. Build.

Vision

To be the studio people trust when they want technology that feels thoughtful, fast and human, built from Afghanistan for the world.

Mission

To turn ideas into polished, working digital products, with clear communication and honest timelines from the first message to launch.

Create

Every project starts with listening. We research your audience and shape a visual identity that is specific to you, not a template.

Innovate

We test new tools, including AI, and use them only when they make the result better, faster or easier to maintain.

Build

Clean code, responsive layouts and fast load times. We ship work that holds up on a phone, a tablet and a laptop.

Why clients choose us

  • Bilingual by default. English and Persian, with correct RTL and LTR layouts.
  • Direct communication. You talk to the people doing the work, on WhatsApp or email.
  • Built for performance. Lightweight pages that load quickly on any connection.
